matching source collections. Its current visibility determines download access;
this file is a source/version mapping, not a live availability check. Source
access must accompany binary access without an extra source-access fee. The

licenses. Original notices and license texts in the portable and installer

Publication repack: application/installer executable source is unchanged.
Application source documents were reduced to public build guidance. Media
distributor metadata and reconstruction helper URLs were repaired, and missing
legal texts recovered where supported by upstream evidence. Original retained
source archives, patches, and legal texts were not edited. Four macOS-only
crates with unresolved standalone notice evidence are omitted from this
Windows-focused collection; lockfiles retain cross-platform entries and the
collection is not a complete offline all-target Cargo source set.

The exact historical supplier librsvg Cargo.lock was not captured. The
reconstructed lock is labelled as reconstructed, and unrecoverable historical
origin URL fields are explicitly unknown rather than invented. Full-cohort
byte-identical rebuilding and independent supplier GPG verification have not
been established. See PUBLICATION-CHANGES.txt and source indexes in each ZIP.

Earlier SOURCE-ACCESS and INSTALLER-SOURCE-ACCESS files embedded in unchanged
binaries describe a private/draft build snapshot and older archive hashes.
This release-side supplement supplies current names, mappings and locations;
it does not alter those original files or claim that the binary was rebuilt.
