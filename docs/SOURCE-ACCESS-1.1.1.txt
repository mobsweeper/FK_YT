Other source and notices
========================
Deno's applicable MPL package sources are already included in the runnable
payload at notices/deno/third-party/sources/mpl/. The accompanying
MPL-SOURCE-AVAILABILITY.txt and component indexes identify them. Preserve these
sources and original notices when redistributing the payload.

The installer uses NSIS 3.13 zlib compression. Its source collection retains
the original NSIS, MUI2 and used-plugin notices, including component exceptions.
The exact toolchain archives and hashes are identified by its build pins.

Historical records and verification limits
=========================================
Original 1.0 and preview notices embedded in retained payloads identify earlier
application names, private/draft status and earlier source archive hashes.
They are preserved as historical records. Use this document and the final
v1.1.1 SHA256SUMS.txt for the current names and source mapping; no historical
notice is rewritten to imply that an older binary was rebuilt.

The external publication repack repaired distributor metadata/origin fields and
added recovered legal notices. Original retained source archives, patches and
legal texts were preserved. Four macOS-only crate blobs with unresolved legal
notice evidence were excluded before this unchanged Windows source collection;
their cross-platform lockfile entries remain. It is not a complete offline
all-target Cargo source set. No additional source exclusions occur for 1.1.1.

The supplier's exact historical final librsvg Cargo.lock was not captured.
The upstream lock and a labelled reconstruction are supplied, with original
recipes and source records. Unrecoverable historical origin URLs are marked
unknown rather than invented. A full external-cohort bit-identical rebuild and
independent supplier-package GPG verification are not established. These are
verification limits, not a claim that such checks passed. See the archives'
PUBLICATION-CHANGES.txt, build controls and source indexes for details.

Source access is supplied through matching files rather than an unfulfilled
promise to provide source later. Modified redistributions must preserve the
applicable notices and supply source corresponding to their own changed
binaries. A link to an upstream latest branch does not identify that source.
